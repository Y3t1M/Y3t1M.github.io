Photo in, 3D-printable drawer organizer out. The desktop app extracts an object's silhouette from a photo and generates an STL tray with a matching pocket. This is the extraction engine, rebuilt in JavaScript — drag the sliders until the outline hugs the object.

How the real app works

The desktop version (Python · OpenCV · CadQuery) estimates the background color from the photo's corners, thresholds the color distance to isolate the object, cleans the mask with morphological passes, and traces the largest contour. That outline gets extruded into a parametric tray in CadQuery and exported as a printable STL.

This page reimplements the extraction stage — corner sampling, color-distance threshold, dilate/erode smoothing — in about 150 lines of vanilla JavaScript on a canvas.
