    Facilities work-order software behind hollisbloom.com, running Integrity Marketing Group's offices at Fountain Place, Dallas.     RH Agentic

    Autonomous agents that analyze market data and execute equity and options trades end to end, reporting to an encrypted dashboard. Nine stages (scan, research, score, validate, execute, manage, learn) with a deterministic risk engine at the centre: a validator DENY is unconditional, and every score, decision and rejection is written to an append-only audit trail. It runs live on my own account; the preview below uses sample data.

    NeatFreak

    Photo in, 3D-printable drawer organizer out. Take a picture of a tool on a plain background; NeatFreak extracts its silhouette, lets you tune the outline with live sliders, and generates an STL tray with a custom-fit pocket ready for the printer. The extraction engine is rebuilt in JavaScript on this site. Try it on your own photo.
